Location: Fringe of the Hybrasil star system
Date: 196.1.6

A "trade convoy" of UNSF ships just made a life-changing deal with a Tri-Tachyon commerce group, by exchanging blueprints for shield generators that rotate power loads, allowing down-time for each to reroute and vent significant built-up, while the others handle shield upkeep. The deal also seemed life-changing to a local Hegemony armada, as they slimed out the defenseless commerce fleet, and now want to make sure no Tri-Tach officer ever gets their hands on these blueprints. By "..blowin' these 'gaters back where they came from." Time to put those shield generators to good use...
